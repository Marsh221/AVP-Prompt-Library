# Red Dragon — Demon Marshal (Donghua-inspired)

**Series reference:** *The Demon Hunter* (Cang Yuan Tu / 沧元图) — Youku, Shenman Entertainment
**Pillar:** Donghua-inspired mythology
**Output:** `art/red-dragon-demon-marshal.png` — cinematic 16:9
**Status:** selected take (option 1 of 2)

## Six-Step Breakdown

### 1. Subject
A colossal ancient Eastern dragon — the red dragon avatar of a Demon Marshal — towering over a ruined mountain sect.

### 2. Appearance
Long serpentine body, **no wings**, branching antler horns, long trailing whiskers, mane of drifting ember-flame, deep crimson and scarlet scales edged in molten gold, four clawed limbs with obsidian talons, eyes burning like red stars, red-black demonic aura smoking off the hide. A tiny human silhouette in dharmic armor on a rooftop for scale.

### 3. Environment
Storm-lit ancient Chinese mountain ruin at night: broken stone pagoda roofs, weathered guardian statues framing the foreground, ridges of pine, drifting mist, ash and embers falling like snow, distant pagodas stacked down the cliffs.

### 4. Camera
Dramatic low-angle wide shot from the ruin courtyard. The dragon's head fills the upper frame, tail spiraling away into cloud, ruins receding into depth on both sides.

### 5. Lighting / Mood
Volumetric crimson rim light on the scales, cold blue moonlight and storm clouds behind, glowing constellation sparks scattered through the air, heavy mist. Epic, oppressive mythological scale.

### 6. Avoid
Western winged dragon, tiny dragon, flat cartoon shading, clutter, distorted anatomy, text, watermark, signature.

## What worked
- Contrast pairing: warm crimson dragon vs. cold blue moon/storm — gives the silhouette separation that flat red-on-red prompts lose.
- Guardian statues in the near foreground create an incidental frame and sell scale harder than the tiny human does.
- "No wings" + antler horns is the single strongest lever separating *Eastern* dragon from generic dragon.
- Constellation sparks (a recurring visual motif in the donghua) push it from generic fantasy into the show's specific look.
