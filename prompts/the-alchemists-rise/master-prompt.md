# The Alchemist's Rise — Master Scene Prompt

A modular prompt system for generating art from any part of **The Alchemist's Rise** (*傲世丹神 / World Defying Dan God*, Ruo Hong Culture × iQIYI, 2025, 12 episodes, 3D donghua).

Built on the six-step framework: **Subject + Appearance + Environment + Camera + Lighting/Mood + Avoid**, with an always-on style anchor and swappable character/scene modules.

---

## Series Research (what the art must capture)

- **Protagonist:** Shen Xiang (沈翔), ~16, born with damaged spiritual veins and dismissed as crippled. Extremely handsome, sword-like brows, deep bright eyes, tall athletic build. Son of Shen Tianhu of the Shen Family, Southern Martial Empire.
- **Inciting quest:** His father is gravely injured. Shen Xiang ventures alone into the Ten Thousand Demon Valley / Immortal Demon Cliff to find a legendary cure (Hell Lingzhi spirit herb).
- **The fateful encounter:** At the bottom of the cliff, shrouded in poisonous black mist beside a dark pool, he finds two wounded women — **Su Meiyao** (苏媚瑶), a divine alchemist goddess of the Nine Heavens, and **Bai Youyou** (白幽幽), the proud Nine Nether demon princess. He saves them.
- **Awakening:** A blood contract binds the three. Their Extreme Yang and Extreme Yin divine veins merge through him, awakening the Heaven-Defying God Vein.
- **Signature duality:** Alchemy by day (pill refining, ancient cauldrons, golden elixir fire), battle by night (spear and sword techniques). Warm gold fire vs. cold silver-blue yin energy is the visual identity of the series.
- **Arc 1 — Jade Seizing:** Pursuit of the mythical Heaven Jade; conflict with the scheming young alchemist Yao Tianhua (药天华) and monstrous tribes.
- **Arc 2 — Taiwu:** The forbidden Taiwu Realm, sacred stones of an ancient fire-deity sect, and the supreme Taiwu Divine Pill — the refinement that crowns him the World-Defying Dan God.
- **Visual style of the show:** Detailed expressive 3D CG models, ancient Chinese architecture and hanfu, ancient bronze cauldrons, celestial star arrays, glowing mystic sigils, fluid alchemical-flame VFX blended with kinetic spear combat.

## 2. Character Modules (include only who appears)

**Shen Xiang (沈翔)** — a handsome 16-year-old martial cultivator, sword-like brows, deep bright eyes, black hair partly tied up, tall athletic build, dark blue-grey and white hanfu robes with subtle silver trim; yin-yang energy of gold (Yang) and ice-silver (Yin) orbiting his arms; determined resilient expression.

**Su Meiyao (苏媚瑶)** — a stunning goddess with long flowing black hair, warm golden-red fire aura, elegant layered robes of crimson and gold, serene alluring beauty, calm pragmatic gaze; soft warm divine light around her.

**Bai Youyou (白幽幽)** — a cold proud demon princess, long pale silver-white hair, icy blue eyes, dark purple-black demoness robes with silver trim, cold true-qi yin mist swirling at her feet, battle-thirsty sharp gaze.

**Xue Xianxian (薛仙仙)** — an ethereal young fairy beauty, long dark hair with jade hairpin, white and pale-blue celestial robes, gentle immortal grace, faint starlight aura.

**Yao Tianhua (药天华)** — an arrogant young alchemist villain, ornate dark green and black robes, sneering expression, murky poisonous green flame in his palms.

**Female Emperor (女帝)** — a majestic empress in layered imperial robes of gold and vermilion, elaborate crown, overwhelming imperial pressure, golden dragon-shaped qi coiling around her.

## 3. Scene Modules (pick the part of the series)

**A. The Desperate Quest** — Shen Xiang alone climbing the demonic cliffs of the Ten Thousand Demon Valley, searching for spirit herbs; jagged black rocks, poisonous violet-black mist, glowing red Hell Lingzhi fungus on the cliff walls.

**B. The Encounter at the Cliff** — Shen Xiang discovering the two wounded women beside a dark mirror-like pool at the bottom of the Immortal Demon Cliff; Su Meiyao and Bai Youyou seated and injured, sacred and demonic auras intertwined in the poisonous mist.

**C. The Awakening / Blood Contract** — the three blood-contracting as twin pillars of gold and ice-silver energy erupt and spiral into a yin-yang vortex around Shen Xiang; his God Vein blazing to life, robes and hair whipping upward.

**D. Pill Refining** — Shen Xiang at a colossal ancient bronze alchemy cauldron, golden elixir flames surging, luminous pills rising on the fire column, celestial star arrays and glowing sigils rotating overhead; Su Meiyao and Bai Youyou flanking him as guides.

**E. Clan Duel** — Shen Xiang mid-leap with a blazing spear against arrogant rival clan youths in a grand stone martial arena of the Southern Martial Empire; banners, crowds, cracked flagstones.

**F. Jade Seizing Arc** — the trio pursuing the floating Heaven Jade through monster-infested ravines; Yao Tianhua and monstrous beast-tribes closing in, green villain flame vs. gold and silver hero energy.

**G. Taiwu Ascension (finale)** — Shen Xiang refining the supreme Taiwu Divine Pill inside the fiery ruins of the ancient fire-deity sect in the forbidden Taiwu Realm; sacred stones orbiting a titanic furnace, god-vein power erupting skyward as he rises to become the World-Defying Dan God.

## 4. Camera Module (pick one)

- `hero shot` — dramatic low-angle wide shot, subject huge against the sky.
- `encounter` — wide establishing shot with the subject small in a vast environment, slow reveal.
- `portrait` — medium close-up, shallow depth of field, cinematic bokeh.
- `action` — dynamic dutch angle, motion blur on energy trails, frozen mid-strike.
- `ascension` — extreme wide, figure centered inside a vertical column of light, symmetrical epic framing.

## 5. Lighting / Mood Module (pick one)

- `quest` — cold desaturated moonlight, eerie suspenseful mood.
- `alchemy` — warm golden furnace glow as key light, ember particles, reverent awe.
- `awakening` — high-contrast dual lighting, gold vs. ice-silver clash, transcendent explosive mood.
- `battle` — hard directional light, dust and sparks, tense ferocious mood.
- `finale` — divine god rays, radiant volumetric light, triumphant sacred mood.

## Avoid (always append)

> Avoid text, watermark, logo, modern objects, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, extra limbs, cluttered composition, flat lighting, chibi proportions, 2D flat anime style, excessive bloom, plastic skin.

## Recipe: Any Other Part of the Series

1. Paste the **Style Anchor**.
2. Pick a **Scene Module** (or write one in the same "Subject doing action + story beat" form).
3. Add only the **Character Modules** present — keep their key colors consistent (Shen Xiang = dark blue/silver + gold-silver qi; Su Meiyao = crimson/gold + warm fire; Bai Youyou = purple-black/silver + cold yin).
4. Pick one **Camera** and one **Lighting/Mood** module.
5. Paste the **Avoid** block.

## Practice Rule (from the core framework)

After generating, do not rewrite everything. Identify the biggest failure, change only the module that controls it (Subject / Appearance / Environment / Camera / Lighting / Avoid), generate again, and compare.
