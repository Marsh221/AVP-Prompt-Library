# Six-Step Image Prompt Framework

A simple framework for building controlled image-generation prompts without making them unnecessarily long.

## 1. Subject
What is the main thing in the image?

**Example:** A colossal ancient Chinese demon.

## 2. Appearance
Describe the important physical characteristics.

**Example:** Huge muscular blue body, dark glowing eyes, intimidating presence.

## 3. Environment
Where is the subject?

**Example:** Vast abandoned Chinese ruins with broken stone structures and drifting mist.

## 4. Camera
How should the viewer see the scene?

**Example:** Dramatic low-angle wide shot emphasizing enormous scale.

## 5. Lighting / Mood
Describe the atmosphere, light, and emotional tone.

**Example:** Dark stormy atmosphere, cold cinematic lighting, eerie ancient mood.

## 6. Avoid
State recurring problems or unwanted elements.

**Example:** Avoid tiny hills, clutter, distorted anatomy, excessive glow, and text.

## Formula
**Subject + Appearance + Environment + Camera + Lighting/Mood + Avoid**

## Complete Example
> A colossal ancient Chinese demon with a huge muscular blue body and dark glowing eyes stands among vast abandoned Chinese ruins. Broken stone structures and drifting mist surround him. Dramatic low-angle wide shot emphasizing his enormous scale. Dark stormy atmosphere with cold cinematic lighting and an eerie ancient mood. Avoid tiny hills, clutter, distorted anatomy, excessive glow, and text.

## Practice Rule
After generating an image, do not rewrite everything immediately.

1. Identify what worked.
2. Identify the biggest failure.
3. Determine which of the six sections controls that failure.
4. Change that section.
5. Generate again and compare.
