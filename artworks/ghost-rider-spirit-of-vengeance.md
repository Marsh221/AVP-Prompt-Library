# GHOST RIDER — Spirit of Vengeance

> **AVP Dark Cinema Collection · Fan-art study · October 2026**

A three-frame visual sequence built around one idea: **fire is not just an effect — it is the character's atmosphere.**

## The sequence

### I. The Summoning — The Rider
A skeletal rider emerges from smoke on a black horse, wrapped in molten amber fire. Keep the figure centered and mostly silhouetted; let the burning skull and rim light do the storytelling.

**Role in the sequence:** opening / strongest character reveal  
**Suggested alt text:** A skeletal rider with a glowing skull sits on a black horse surrounded by orange flames in a dark, smoky scene.

### II. The Inferno — The Horse
A tight, aggressive view of the flaming horse's head. Strong directional motion, blown-out white-hot highlights, deep blacks, and ember-orange streaks create the feeling of raw speed.

**Role in the sequence:** impact / energy  
**Suggested alt text:** A horse's head engulfed in bright orange and white fire against a nearly black night sky.

### III. The Escape — The Machine
A lone rider tears across an open road at dusk, leaving a long ribbon of fire behind the motorcycle. The wide negative space makes the flame trail feel enormous.

**Role in the sequence:** closing frame / motion  
**Suggested alt text:** A dark motorcycle rider speeds across a road at dusk with a long, bright trail of fire behind the bike.

## Art direction

- **Palette:** obsidian black, smoke teal, burnt amber, molten orange, white-hot highlights.
- **Lighting:** fire as the key light; strong rim light; keep the shadows rich instead of lifting everything.
- **Texture:** subtle film grain, smoky atmosphere, realistic flame detail, restrained motion blur.
- **Composition:** one clear focal point per frame; preserve negative space; avoid clutter.
- **Sequence rhythm:** reveal → impact → escape.
