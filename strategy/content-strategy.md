## Findings

1. **The phoenix reel broke out of the follower bubble.** 94.4% non-follower views means Instagram is testing the content with cold audiences. That's the moment to feed the algorithm more of the same — not pause.
2. **Format validated.** ~7s micro-motion loop + cosmic dark fantasy = 8.1% interaction rate with saves/shares at 20% of interactions. This is the format combo to repeat.
3. **Pinterest is the discovery engine; crossover niches are the volume.** F1 pin = 159.9k impressions at 3.4% ER. The account-wide ↓16% is a *new-pin flow* problem, not a quality problem: Pinterest is a search engine where every pin is a doorway, and the doorways age when the flow stops.
4. **Leaky bucket.** Reaching ~3.7k accounts but only 2,832 followers. Fix = serialized content (reason to follow) + CTA in captions + a bio that states the promise ("new cosmic fantasy series weekly").

## Platform roles

- **Instagram** — storytelling, followers, momentum. Best for: reels, carousels, story arcs.
- **Pinterest** — evergreen search discovery, compounds over time. Best for: 2:3 image pins, video pins, keyword titles.
- **Rule: every asset ships to both.** Reel → IG reel + Pinterest video pin. Still → IG post/carousel + Pinterest 2:3 pin.

## Content pillars

1. **Cosmic / dark fantasy arcs** — "Ascend into the Light" / phoenix. The IG breakout engine. Run in arcs of 2–3 pieces, each a still + 7s reel.
2. **Crossover search niches** — F1 / motorsport. The Pinterest volume engine (159k impressions). 1–2 pieces/month.
3. **Donghua-inspired mythology** — ice phoenix / Frozen End Divine Arts direction. Identity pillar + a different audience from pillar 1.

## Format rule (from own data)

Every piece = **1 still** (IG post + Pinterest 2:3 pin) + **1 ~7s micro-motion video** (IG reel + Pinterest video pin).

## Cadence

- **IG:** 4–5 posts/week, at least 2 reels. Test slots 1–3pm and 7–9pm EAT; confirm with Insights → Total followers → Most active times.
- **Pinterest:** 5–10 pins/week — fresh pins + re-pinning top performers to keep the flow.
- **Series:** every arc ends with a CTA ("follow for part 2").

## Caption / hook patterns

- Story hook: "It took a thousand years to master. It took one night to wake it up."
- CTA question: "Which creature should I bring back to life next?"
- Follow CTA: "Follow for Part 2."
- Hashtags: 5–8 targeted (#darkfantasy #cosmicart #digitalart #phoenix #conceptart #aivisuals #donghua) — not 30.

## Pinterest SEO

- Pins at 2:3 (1000×1500) — crop or outpaint the stills.
- Title = search phrase + art term: "Phoenix Rising — Dark Fantasy Cosmic Digital Art".
- Description: 2–3 sentences with natural keywords; link to the IG profile.
- Thematic boards: Cosmic Fantasy · Motorsport Art · Donghua Mythology.
